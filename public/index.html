If your phone says "Cannot Connect" or times out:

Windows Firewall may be blocking incoming connections on port 5000.

Run the included 1-click script in the app folder:

allow-firewall.bat

Or in PowerShell as Admin: netsh advfirewall firewall add rule name="MyDocs" dir=in action=allow protocol=TCP localport=5000
